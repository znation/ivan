The Holy Stack of Valpuris,
He who carrieth our flat universe, so that it won't sink into the Sea of Eternity.

Edition according to the Holymango-Hexist sect of the Valpurian Cathedral.

Much of the canonical Stack is in the old tongue (e.g. the writings of the prophet and
clairvoyant Vesa, the Gospel of Timoteus and the Third Book of Timo), so this is but a
torso and inadequate to convey the True Image of the Full Glory of Valpuris. We apologize
for the inconvenience.
