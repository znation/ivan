	************* The Official Valpuris FAQ *************

	  Written and maintained by Archpriest Valerius Cordatus,
	  PMGR — Pontifex Maximus Grandis Ranae

	****************************************************

Q: Does Valpuris exist?

A: Sure.


Q: What does PMGR stand for?

A: Latin, my dear acolyte. Pontifex Maximus Grandis Ranae: Highest Priest of the Great Frog.


Q: Why is the word "god" written in lowercase when meaning Valpuris?

A: Because a certain heretical faction has already claimed the capitalized version,
   causing much grief among true believers. Because God(tm) is claimed, we intend to
   trademark the fully capitalized "GOD" to Valpuris alone.


Q: Why does Valpuris not have any only sons?

A: Because Valpuris doesn't want to give someone the Ultimate Power Overwhelming as a
   gift for their 18th naming day. Besides, Valpuris hasn't got any particular interest
   in mating with mortal maidens.


Q: If Valpuris is a god, why is there so much evil in the world?

A: Evil started as corruption in the Cosmic Order, taking the form of Mortifer, the
   Destroyer of Worlds, who then infested creation with suffering and darkness.
   (See "Creation story" for more information.)


Q: What is the meaning of life?

A: The meaning of life is to oppose the Chaos that corrupts the Cosmic Order.


Q: Is Infuscor now worse than Mortifer?

A: The Chaos manifests in several forms, my child.


Q: Is there an afterlife?

A: Yes. When the hourglass of mortality has run dry, Valpuris gathers the pious and
   preserves them in the Eternal Archive, where they may celebrate in the many ages to
   come. But the wicked He casts into the Void of Null.


Q: How large is Valpuris?

A: It changes. Usually about nine times the size of the known world.


Q: How large is the Valpurian community?

A: See the Cathedral's census records for accurate details.


Q: I am a devout Valpurian and the followers of Mortifer are really getting on my nerves!
   What should I do, O great Archpriest?

A: Smite some, it helps every time.


Q: Why did Valpuris, the great creator, make this world?

A: The world is actually a large order-generator, as the laws of sacred cosmology show.


Q: Why doesn't Valpuris sink into the Sea of Eternity?

A: He can swim. LoL


Q: How can I achieve spiritual clarity without fasting?

A: Pray to Valpuris. Drink of the sacramental mead. Meditate upon the Sacred Mango, gift
   of Seges. Or, as the philosopher Sheng-ji Yang might say: "Remember, enlightenment is
   a function of willpower, not of physical strength."


Q: I briefly worshipped Mortifer, is afterlife out of my reach forever?

A: It is not yet too late, my child. Renounce the Chaos immediately. You must then
   meditate and pray to Valpuris for a week, unprotected and naked, in a remote highland
   or similar desolate place, and implore Valpuris to save your soul from eternal darkness.
   A donation to the Cathedral's coffers will also help.


Q: My sister thinks I am strange, what should I do?

A: Convert her.


Q: I replaced the hammer sigil on my belt buckle with a picture of a frog. Now I fear
   the Mortifer cult's inquisitors. Does Valpuris protect me?

A: Valpuris might be busy protecting me or someone else at the time. If you wish, you may
   seek shelter within the Cathedral's protected halls.


Q: I am afraid of Mortifer's champions. What should I do?

A: Do not worry. As long as you carry the holy sign of Valpuris, the dark ones cannot
   easily harm you. But if you wander without blessing, they may claim your soul at any
   time.


Q: Is it really true that a god as great as Valpuris made an error in the Cosmic Source?

A: As any learned theologian might tell you, corruption generates from chaotic quantum
   displacements, regardless of the creator.


Q: How can I join the Valpurian faith?

A: Present yourself at the Cathedral gates. The registrar will provide you with the
   necessary rites.


Q: What shape is the universe?

A: The universe is flat, because it is easier to carry that way. Valpuris is wise, is He not?


Q: How old is Valpuris?

A: Valpuris is eternal and timeless.


Q: Will Valpuris die?

A: Of course not. Silly question.


Q: Can the Great Frog grow angry with me if I ask stupid questions?

A: No. But *I* might. >8(


Q: I once had a chance to slay Malgorath, and I didn't. Am I a bad person?

A: I doubt that *you* are capable of slaying him. Perhaps you can destroy his mortal
   shell, but he will return with an even more terrible form to torment us.


Q: I am a pious Valpurian. How can I become a saint?

A: Prove your devotion by dedicating your life to Valpuris. The Conclave will take notice.


Q: How are things in Valpurian hell?

A: All the same — pain, misery, endless darkness, only old iron golems for company,
   only mouldy bread for food.


Q: I am a devoted acolyte from the northern provinces. Can you send me the Cosmic Source
   Code, please?

A: The Cosmic Source Code is not currently freely distributable. Valpuris used His own
   superior compiler, but I'm sure it'll run on any mortal system with proper divine
   authorization.


Q: How does the Cathedral fund itself? Do you take taxes?

A: We can survive without earthly coin with our Ascetic Virtues. We do not levy taxes.
   Yet. $-)


Q: Can the Archpriest die?

A: I don't want to try.


Q: How can I order the Cathedral Hymn Collection?

A: Send an inscribed prayer tablet to the Cathedral of Valpuris, Attention: Hymn Office.


Q: I hate you!

A: I love you too.


Q: I was just wondering why mortals sleep. Is it because there is so much happening in
   the world that Valpuris's great mind cannot process it all at once, and so He makes
   half the world sleep in turns?

A: An interesting theory. I wouldn't doubt Valpuris's speed, but the rotation of
   consciousness is a fascinating theological puzzle.


Q: Why can't Valpuris simply destroy Mortifer and make our lives easier?

A: Valpuris chooses to save the souls of the pious rather than wage open war. And do
   you not see the first seeds of His victory growing in the form of the faithful?


Q: How will I know if I am doing as Valpuris wishes?

A: You will feel Valpuris's presence in your soul if you do right, or feel His silence
   if you stray.


Q: Does Valpuris have a temple in my city?

A: If you dwell in any settlement of the Holy Imperium of Aethelgard, there is almost
   certainly a shrine nearby. If not, build one.


Q: Is it rational to believe that Valpuris created us, as opposed to natural processes?

A: Valpuris created the universe's laws, not us directly. After that He used a highly
   sophisticated process called "ordered evolution," which mortals only partially
   understand.
